Navigation & VR Guide

🖥️ Desktop Orbit Controls

  • Rotate: Left Mouse Drag
  • Pan: Right Mouse Drag / Two-finger swipe
  • Zoom: Mouse Scroll Wheel / Pinch
  • Auto-Frame: Click Fit View or press F

🚶 First-Person / Walk Mode

  • Look: Move Mouse (click canvas to lock cursor)
  • Move: W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • Sprint: Hold Shift
  • Elevate / Descend: Space / C
  • Exit Walk Mode: Press Esc

🥽 Virtual Reality (WebXR) Controls

  • Enter VR: Click the "ENTER VR" button in the top bar.
  • Teleportation: Pull and hold Right or Left Trigger to aim arc, release to teleport instantly.
  • Smooth Locomotion: Push Left Thumbstick forward/backward/sides to walk smoothly.
  • Snap / Smooth Turn: Flick Right Thumbstick left/right to snap rotate.
  • Works with: Meta Quest 2/3/Pro, Apple Vision Pro, PCVR (Oculus/Index/Vive), and WebXR browser emulators.
